🦄 Rainbow Reveal

Use WASD or the arrow keys (touch-drag on mobile) to move your unicorn across the dark canvas — its glowing trail reveals a hidden picture underneath.

Press Enter or tap the ? button to guess what it is.

You have 3 lives — a wrong guess costs one. Guess right and a new picture loads. Fewer moves = better score.
